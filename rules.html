Official Server Rules

By playing on SelyxCraft, you agree to follow these rules.

1 Respect & Conduct

In-game & Discord
  1. 1.1Treat all players, staff, and the community with respect.
  2. 1.2Harassment, threats, discrimination (including racism or sexism), and toxic behavior are prohibited.
  3. 1.3Swearing is allowed unless used in a derogatory or targeted manner.
  4. 1.4False accusations, intentional misinformation, and serious griefing are prohibited.
  5. 1.5Use common sense.

What this means

Survival is semi-anarchy. Raiding and stealing are part of the game. Serious griefing gets punished: mass destruction with lava or TNT, wiping out entire bases or spawn, and lag or crash builds.

2 Advertising

In-game & Discord
  1. 2.1Advertising other Minecraft servers is strictly prohibited.
  2. 2.2Sharing server IPs, Discord invites, or promotional links is not allowed.
  3. 2.3Mentioning large public servers (e.g., Hypixel) casually is allowed.
  4. 2.4YouTube links may only be shared in designated channels. Discord

3 Channel Usage

Discord
  1. 3.1Use channels only for their intended purpose.
  2. 3.2Off-topic content may be removed without notice.

4 Unfair Advantage

In-game

The following are strictly prohibited:

  1. 4.1Modified clients or software that provide gameplay advantages beyond vanilla Minecraft
  2. 4.2Exploiting bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics
  3. 4.3Cross-teaming in team-based modes
  4. 4.4Knowingly playing with cheaters
  5. 4.5Lag machines or server-crashing mechanisms
  6. 4.6If you discover a bug, report it instead of abusing it.

What this means

Allowed: performance and visual mods, like Sodium, Iris, OptiFine, shaders, and resource packs.

Banned: x-ray (mods or resource packs), minimaps and world maps, entity or cave radar, freecam, hacked clients, auto-clickers, and macros.

5 Account Responsibility

In-game & Discord
  1. 5.1Account sharing, selling, or transferring is prohibited.
  2. 5.2You are fully responsible for actions taken on your account.
  3. 5.3Any punishments applied to your account remain valid regardless of who was using it.

What this means

Alts are only allowed for AFK farms or keeping chunks loaded. Nothing else.

6 Chat Rules

In-game & Discord
  1. 6.1No spam or flooding outside designated channels.
  2. 6.2No NSFW or sexually explicit content.
  3. 6.3Do not tag users excessively or without reason.
  4. 6.4No hate speech or discriminatory remarks.
  5. 6.5Keep discussions appropriate for a 13+ community.

7 Enforcement

In-game & Discord
  1. 7.1Staff decisions are final.
  2. 7.2Punishments may include mutes, bans, rank removal, or permanent suspension.
  3. 7.3Punishment evasion (including alt accounts) may result in permanent bans.

8 Rule Changes

In-game & Discord
  1. 8.1SelyxCraft reserves the right to modify these rules at any time.
  2. 8.2Continued participation after changes means you accept the updated rules.

Not sure if something's allowed?

Ask in a ticket on Discord before you do it. Appeals and bug reports go there too.
