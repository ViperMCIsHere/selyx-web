    FAQ

    When does SelyxCraft launch?

    Soon. The launch date and server IP get announced on Discord first, so join there to catch it.

    What version do I need?

    Java Edition 26.2+. Bedrock Edition is supported too.

    Can I join with a cracked account?

    Yes. Cracked (non-premium) accounts are allowed.

    Are there land claims?

    No. Survival is semi-anarchy: raiding and stealing are part of the game, so hide your base well. Serious griefing is still punished, see rule 1.4.

    Is PvP on?

    Yes, everywhere except spawn. Spawn is a safe zone.

    Which mods can I use?

    Performance and visual mods only, like Sodium, Iris, or shaders. Minimaps, x-ray, and anything else that gives an advantage are banned. Full list under rule 4.1.

    Can I use alts?

    Only for AFK farms or keeping chunks loaded. Using an alt for anything else, including dodging a punishment, isn't allowed. See rule 5.

    Does the world reset?

    Once a year, when a new season starts. The world and economy are wiped. Purchased ranks and cosmetics carry over.

    I got banned or muted. How do I appeal?

    Open a ticket on Discord. Staff review every appeal, and their decision is final.

    I found a bug. What now?

    Report it on Discord instead of using it. Abusing bugs is against rule 4.6.
